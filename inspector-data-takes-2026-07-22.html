Turning the technical dump into packets

The right panel is word soup because ~20 fields of different types — IDs, paths, enums, counts, timestamps, nulls — are all rendered as identical grey key/value lines, and the controls block repeats its one reason four times. Same five moves fix it; three takes below spend them differently. All render the real "Orchestrate a CMUX swarm" session.

move 01Render by typeNumbers get tabular alignment + bars; IDs truncate with copy; nulls recede instead of shouting.
move 02Group by meaningIdentity · Runtime · Consumption · Verification · Control — not one flat list.
move 03Rank by attentionThe 2–3 numbers you act on lead; reference facts you only look up recede.
move 04Compute the insight"in 142k · cached 140k" → 98% cached. "142k / 250k" → a fill bar. Show the ratio, not the raw pair.
move 05Say it onceFour controls, one shared reason line — not the quarantine sentence pasted four times.
