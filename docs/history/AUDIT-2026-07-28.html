BunCommandRunner timeout kills the child but never settles the promise — one hang wedges the entire hub forever

src/server/command.ts:8
What breaks

cmux RPC (cmux rpc debug.terminals), ps, and lsof all route through this one method. Add a direct BunCommandRunner test that spawns sh -c 'trap "" TERM; sleep 60' and asserts run() settles with timedOut:true inside the budget.

After the fix A hung cmux/ps/lsof invocation degrades one tick instead of freezing the dashboard permanently. `generatedAt` never drifts more than a few ticks behind wall clock.
