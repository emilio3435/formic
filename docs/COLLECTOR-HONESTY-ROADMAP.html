03 · Future direction

Evidence first. Then expand.

The next chapter is not “fill every cell.” It is to shorten the distance between a new source fact and a verified collector while keeping every fail-closed boundary intact.

01 / observe

Run the two bounded probes

Capture a representative Antigravity legacy fixture and one real local Muse store. If either format cannot support a deterministic parser, record that and stop.

02 / monitor

Watch source formats, not symptoms

Look for authored roles, cumulative totals, call boundaries, exact process identity, per-turn clocks, authored titles, and child creation times.

03 / ship

Take the ready work

Replace the Hermes Formic-mark stand-in with the official Nous Research mark. Add Cline, OpenCode, or Amp only when each can meet the full parity bar.

04 / defend

Keep the contract executable

Every format change lands with a fixture, collector update, failing-on-fake test, and same-commit ledger revision. No heuristic gets a grace period.

✓
Success is a smaller honest ledger, not a fuller-looking board.

Strike a row only when direct evidence fills it. Preserve guardrails when the correct answer is still refusal, absence, or unknown.
